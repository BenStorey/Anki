# Pending Pile Tracker — Anki add-on + history backfill

A tiny Anki add-on that records the **total number of pending review cards**
(limits ignored) at the start of each Anki day, plus a revlog-based
**backfill** of ~12 months of history, a **deck-by-deck daily diff** on the
deck browser, and a **deck-by-deck history chart in the stats screen**.

## Goal (Ben's)

Ben hasn't reviewed in ~12 months and has a huge backlog. He wants to see the
pending pile **shrink over time**: total pending count (NOT respecting deck
daily limits — the 150/day limit is just a pacing tool; the number he wants
is the true outstanding total), how much it dropped since yesterday, and a
chart.

- Vaguely accurate history is fine (scheduler changed over time).
- Very accurate going forward (live snapshot every day he opens Anki).
- Deck-by-deck breakdown in the history chart, not just one global number.
- All reading must be read-only; the add-on never touches scheduling.

**DECIDED: pending = review cards only (type 2, queue >= 0, due <= today's
day number). New cards are EXCLUDED from the headline number** (Ben has
15,705 unreviewed new cards whose count doesn't change meaningfully day to
day — including them would drown the review-progress signal). Learning/new
counts are still *logged* as extra CSV columns for completeness, but the
headline, diff banner, and chart use review-only.

## The Type-5 hole: FSRS mass reschedule (DECIDED 2026-11-24, verified)

Ben ran FSRS manually from within Anki in Dec 2024/Jan 2025 (5 event-days;
50,447 type-5 revlog entries on ~26.7k cards). A type-5 entry records the
NEW interval but NOT the due that was actually written: across the
26,735 cards whose last entry is the reschedule and were never reviewed
since (so cards.due IS ground truth), `due == t5day + ivl` holds for only
0.1% — and every other estimator (t5day+lastIvl, prevday+ivl,
first-review lastIvl) fails too. **The pre-reschedule dues are NOT
recoverable by estimation.** They are, however, exactly recoverable by
observation, because the reschedule output is still sitting in the cards
table for any card not reviewed afterwards.

**Rules (all verified against live data):**

1. **Backfill window starts at Anki-day 4472** (the day after the last
   type-5 event = 2025-01-23 03:00 HKT). That gives 671 days of history
   (more than the 400 originally requested) with NO type-5 events inside
   the window, so gap-fill math is exact throughout.
2. Backward-walking a card within the window: entries of type 4/5 are
   opaque due-writers. When the walk crosses one:
   - if the card has **no review (1/2/3) after it**: use the card's
     CURRENT `card.due` (read from the live DB at backfill time) — that
     IS what the rescheduler wrote, exact.
   - if the card **has a later review**: due between the reschedule and
     that review is unknowable — count the card as pending from the
     reschedule day until that review (conservative upper bound).
3. Never-reviewed cards are NOT pending-review (they're new — excluded
   per Ben's decision). This also applies to reschedule-only cards with
   zero revlog: excluded until their first real review.

**Validation (real run, 2026-11-24):** reconstructed vs live at day 5142
= 14,615 vs 14,616 (−1 card, 0.007%). Per-deck: Japanese 8124/8124,
Chinese 5785/5785, Japanese Proper Nouns 686/686 exact; Korean Vocab −1
(the collection's single suspended card is a day-learner, invisible to
the review rule — acceptable); Japanese (Old) and Spanish reconstruct 0
vs their true 0 pending (the 123/33 figures in the original deck list
were total review cards, not pending — see corrected numbers above).
The naive rule WITHOUT the type-5 handling scores 21,179 vs 14,616
(+45%) and fails the gate — the type-5 rules above are what makes the
gate pass.

## Gap handling: days when Anki was never opened (DECIDED)

Ben confirmed: the number is always **as-of before any reviews that day**
(first open of the day wins). Days he doesn't open Anki at all produce no
snapshot — but they are **exactly reconstructible**, so no gaps remain in
the series:

- **Key property:** `P(N) = P(N−1) + D(N) − R(N−1)` where
  - `P(N)` = pending review cards at start of Anki-day N,
  - `D(N)` = cards whose due day == N (they flip from future-scheduled to
    pending at the day boundary — deterministic from card due dates),
  - `R(N−1)` = distinct pending cards reviewed during day N−1 (from revlog;
    0 on days Anki never opened).
- So on a no-open day, `P(N) = P(N−1) + D(N)` — exact, no approximation.
- Implementation: the add-on, on first open of a day, ALSO fills any gap
  since the last snapshot (walk missed days, applying the property with
  revlog-verified "no reviews happened" and card due dates), marking those
  rows `source=gapfill`. Same logic as the offline backfill, reused.
- The add-on snapshot is therefore an **anchor** that periodically pins the
  reconstruction to ground truth (catching drift from scheduler changes,
  manual set-due-date, FSRS postpone, etc. — operations without revlog
  entries). Gaps between anchors are pure revlog math.
- If the gapfill's computed value for the anchor day differs materially
  from the live snapshot, log a warning (drift happened — probably a
  non-revlog scheduling operation) and trust the live snapshot from that
  point forward.

## Deliverables

### 1. Add-on `pending_pile/addon/`

Hooks (all failures caught + logged to addon's pending-pile.log, never
crash Anki):

- **Daily snapshot + gap fill:** on `collection_did_load` (+ `sync_did_finish`),
  if the last CSV line's date < today's Anki-day date:
  1. gap-fill missed days (`source=gapfill`, exact via the property above),
  2. append today's live snapshot rows (`source=live`).
  CSV schema: `date,deck,pending_review,pending_learning,pending_new,source`
  One row per **top-level deck** (children folded in by `::` name prefix) +
  an `ALL` row. Pending review SQL (limits-ignored):
  `type=2 AND queue>=0 AND due <= current_day_number`.
- **Deck-browser diff banner:** `gui_hooks.deck_browser_will_render_content`
  → inject one line at top:
  `Pending pile: 14,616 review cards — yesterday: 14,768 (−152, −1.0%)`
  (yesterday's numbers from the CSV). Global-only for v1; per-deck lives in
  the stats chart.
- **Stats chart — deck-by-deck history (Ben's explicit requirement):**
  a "Pending Pile" panel on the stats screen rendering the merged CSV:
  - One line per **top-level deck** (dynamically enumerated — currently 6
    non-empty ones; zero-pending decks get flat-at-zero lines), plus a
    bold total line.
  - Hover/tooltip or a small side table showing per-deck daily diff for the
    selected/last day ("Japanese −120, Chinese −30 …").
  - Inline SVG in the stats WebView (no matplotlib, no external deps).
  - Y = cards, X = dates, backfilled history and live snapshots merged
    (live wins on overlap).

### 2. Backfill `pending_pile/backfill_from_revlog.py`

- Read-only open: `sqlite3.connect("file:...?mode=ro&immutable=1", uri=True)`
- For each card, walk revlog rows in time order (window: Anki-day 4472 →
  today, i.e. ~670 days — NOT "last 400 days"; the earlier window start
  would cut into the type-5 blind zone):
  - after review at ms `t` with `ivl > 0` (days): pending again at
    `t + ivl*86400_000`
  - `ivl <= 0`: pending from `t` (sub-day approximation)
  - type 4/5 entries: opaque due-writers — apply the Type-5 Hole rules
    (use current cards.due when no later review exists; else pending from
    the type-4/5 day until the next review)
  - card starts existing at `cards.id` (epoch ms)
  - A card is "pending review" on day N if it has ≥1 review-type (1/2/3)
    revlog entry before N AND its next review/relearn entry is after N AND
    (the ivl-based due date of the previous entry) <= N. Never-reviewed
    cards are NOT pending-review (they're new — excluded per Ben's
    decision).
- Output per-day, per-top-level-deck pending review counts at each Anki-day
  boundary from day 4472 to today, same CSV schema, `source=backfill`.
- **Validation:** backfill's last-day total vs first live snapshot — print
  both, must match within a few percent. Verified achievable: −1 card of
  14,616 (see Type-5 Hole validation). Deck-level drift too.

## Resolved decisions (from Ben, 2026-11-24)

1. Snapshot = **first open of the day** (before any reviews). Later re-opens
   same Anki day don't overwrite.
2. Headline number = review cards only; new/learning logged as CSV columns.
3. **Deck-by-deck history is required in the chart** (all top-level decks
   dynamically enumerated, zero-pending decks get flat-at-zero lines),
   with per-deck daily diffs.
4. Days with no Anki open are gap-filled exactly via the P(N) property.
5. Backfill window starts at Anki-day 4472 (2025-01-23 HKT), ~670 days,
   with the Type-5 Hole rules — NOT the original "~400 days, ivl-only"
   rule (that scored +45% vs live and fails the validation gate).
6. Pre-reschedule dues are not estimable (verified against every candidate
   formula); they are read exactly from cards.due for never-reviewed-since
   cards, and conservatively bounded for reschedule-then-reviewed cards.
7. FSRS was run manually from within Anki (Ben confirms) — no local agent
   was involved; nothing in src/ touches rescheduling.
