Nuance_EN: Means to bear a physical load or to carry something abstract, such as culture, memory, or hope. Often used in formal or figurative contexts.
Nuance_CN: 既可指承受具体的重量或负荷，也可指承载抽象的事物，如文化、记忆、希望等。常用于正式或书面表达。
Example1: 这座桥每天承载着成千上万辆汽车。
Example1_EN: This bridge carries tens of thousands of cars every day.
Example2: 文字承载着文化和记忆，代代相传。
Example2_EN: Writing carries culture and memory, passed down from generation to generation.
Example3: 他的肩膀承载着全家的希望。
Example3_EN: His shoulders bear the hopes of the whole family.

===word: 破水===
Meaning: waters break; amniotic fluid releases
Nuance_EN: A medical/childbirth term referring to the rupture of the amniotic sac, causing fluid to leak. Used in pregnancy and delivery contexts.
Nuance_CN: 医学术语，指分娩前羊膜破裂、羊水流出。用于怀孕和分娩相关场景。
Example1: 她凌晨破水了，家人赶紧送她去医院。
Example1_EN: Her waters broke in the early morning, and her family rushed her to the hospital.
Example2: 破水后要尽量平躺，并尽快联系医生。
Example2_EN: After the waters break, you should lie down as much as possible and contact the doctor quickly.
Example3: 孕妇破水是临产的重要信号之一。
Example3_EN: A pregnant woman's waters breaking is one of the important signs of labor.

===word: 宝剑===
Meaning: sword
Nuance_EN: A fine or precious sword, often associated with martial arts, heroes, or historical contexts. More literary and dignified than the general word 刀.
Nuance_CN: 指珍贵或锋利的剑，常与武侠、英雄、历史等语境相关，比“刀”更具文学色彩。
Example1: 他收藏了一把古代宝剑。
Example1_EN: He collected an ancient sword.
Example2: 宝剑锋从磨砺出，梅花香自苦寒来。
Example2_EN: The edge of a sword comes from sharpening; the fragrance of plum blossoms comes from bitter cold.
Example3: 侠客腰间佩着一把宝剑，威风凛凛。
Example3_EN: The swordsman wore a sword at his waist, looking imposing.

===word: 飞沫===
Meaning: droplets; splattered drops
Nuance_EN: Small liquid particles that fly through the air, such as from coughing, sneezing, or splashing. Often used in health contexts.
Nuance_CN: 指飞溅或喷出的微小液滴，如咳嗽、打喷嚏或液体飞溅时产生。常用于卫生和健康语境。
Example1: 咳嗽或打喷嚏时会产生大量飞沫。
Example1_EN: Coughing or sneezing produces many droplets.
Example2: 飞沫传播是呼吸道疾病扩散的主要途径之一。
Example2_EN: Droplet transmission is one of the main routes for the spread of respiratory diseases.
Example3: 说话时飞沫四溅会让人感到不适。
Example3_EN: Spraying saliva while talking can make people uncomfortable.

===word: 足足===
Meaning: a full; a whole; no less than
Nuance_EN: An adverb used to emphasize that an amount or duration is surprisingly large or complete. Often carries a tone of emphasis or mild surprise.
Nuance_CN: 副词，强调数量或时间达到令人意外或明显的程度，常带有“竟然有这么多/这么久”的语气。
Example1: 他足足等了一个小时，对方才出现。
Example1_EN: He waited a full hour before the other person showed up.
Example2: 这个西瓜足足有十斤重。
Example2_EN: This watermelon weighs a full ten jin (5 kg).
Example3: 她足足睡了一整天，才恢复过来。
Example3_EN: She slept a whole day before recovering.

===word: 解体===
Meaning: to disintegrate; to break up; to decompose
Nuance_EN: Refers to the breakdown or dissolution of an organic body, a system, an organization, or a country. Can be physical or abstract.
Nuance_CN: 指有机体分解，或社会系统、组织、国家等瓦解、分裂。既可指具体事物，也可指抽象结构。
Example1: 苏联在1991年正式解体。
Example1_EN: The Soviet Union formally disintegrated in 1991.
Example2: 尸体在自然条件下会逐渐解体。
Example2_EN: A body will gradually decompose under natural conditions.
Example3: 旧的社会结构开始解体，新的秩序正在形成。
Example3_EN: The old social structure began to disintegrate, and a new order was taking shape.
