===word: 笑纳===
Meaning: kindly accept (a gift)
Nuance_EN: 笑纳 is a humble expression used when offering a gift, asking the recipient to accept it with a smile. It's polite and modest.
Nuance_CN: 笑纳是送礼时的谦辞，表示请对方笑着收下礼物，语气恭敬客气。
Example1: 这是一点小礼物，请您笑纳。
Example1_EN: This is a small gift; please kindly accept it.
Example2: 区区薄礼，不成敬意，还望笑纳。
Example2_EN: This modest gift is not a token of my respect; I hope you will kindly accept it.
Example3: 客户送了一盒茶叶，说“请笑纳”。
Example3_EN: The client gave a box of tea, saying, "Please accept it with a smile."

===word: 考虑不周===
Meaning: thoughtless, inconsiderate
Nuance_EN: 考虑不周 is an admission that one's planning or decision was not thorough enough, often used as an apology for oversight.
Nuance_CN: 考虑不周指考虑不够全面，常用于道歉或承认疏忽的场合。
Example1: 这件事是我考虑不周，对不起。
Example1_EN: I was thoughtless about this matter; I'm sorry.
Example2: 方案中有些细节考虑不周，需要修改。
Example2_EN: Some details in the plan are ill-considered and need revision.
Example3: 他承认自己考虑不周，导致项目延期。
Example3_EN: He admitted that his lack of thorough consideration caused the project delay.

===word: 有数===
Meaning: have a clear understanding, know the situation
Nuance_EN: 有数 means to know exactly how things stand, often implying confidence in handling a situation. It's used in both positive and cautionary contexts.
Nuance_CN: 有数指对情况心中有数，知道该怎么做，常表示有把握或提醒注意分寸。
Example1: 这件事我心里有数，你放心。
Example1_EN: I have a clear understanding of this matter; rest assured.
Example2: 喝酒要有数，别喝太多。
Example2_EN: Know your limits when drinking; don't drink too much.
Example3: 他对自己未来的规划很有数。
Example3_EN: He has a clear plan for his future.
