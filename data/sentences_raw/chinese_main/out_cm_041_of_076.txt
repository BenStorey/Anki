Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to be deep in thought, often lost in contemplation. It implies a state of focused thinking, sometimes with a quiet or serious atmosphere.
Nuance_CN: 指深沉地思考，常描述陷入思索的状态，气氛安静而专注。可用于阅读、思考问题等场景。
Example1: 他坐在窗前沉思，一动不动。
Example1_EN: He sat by the window, deep in thought, without moving.
Example2: 经过一番沉思，他终于下定了决心。
Example2_EN: After some meditation, he finally made up his mind.
Example3: 老师问了一个问题，全班陷入沉思。
Example3_EN: The teacher asked a question, and the whole class fell into deep thought.

===word: 阔气===
Meaning: luxurious; extravagant
Nuance_EN: An adjective describing someone's spending or lifestyle as showy and lavish, often with a connotation of ostentation. It can also describe things as being of high quality or expensive.
Nuance_CN: 形容花钱大方、排场大或物品奢华，常带有一点炫耀的意味，语气中性偏贬。
Example1: 他请客一向很阔气，每次都去高级餐厅。
Example1_EN: He always treats guests lavishly, taking them to upscale restaurants every time.
Example2: 这栋别墅装修得非常阔气。
Example2_EN: This villa is decorated very luxuriously.
Example3: 不要为了摆阔气而过度消费。
Example3_EN: Don't overspend just to show off.

===word: 交叉===
Meaning: intersect; cross
Nuance_EN: A verb meaning two or more things to cross each other or meet at a point. It can be used for lines, roads, disciplines, or concepts. Neutral term.
Nuance_CN: 指线条、道路、学科等相互交叉或重叠。通用动词，中性。可用于空间或抽象概念。
Example1: 两条主干道在这个路口交叉。
Example1_EN: Two main roads cross at this intersection.
Example2: 他们的研究领域有交叉。
Example2_EN: Their research fields overlap.
Example3: 他把手臂交叉在胸前，不说话。
Example3_EN: He crossed his arms over his chest and said nothing.
