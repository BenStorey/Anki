Nuance_CN: 指通过直接观察或感受就能理解，不需要复杂推理；常用于描述图表、模型等易理解的方式。
Example1: 这张图给人一种很直观的感觉。
Example1_EN: This diagram gives people a very intuitive feeling.
Example2: 直观的教学方法能帮助小学生更快理解概念。
Example2_EN: Intuitive teaching methods can help elementary students understand concepts faster.
Example3: 数据用图表展示后显得更加直观。
Example3_EN: After presenting the data with a chart, it became more directly perceptible.
