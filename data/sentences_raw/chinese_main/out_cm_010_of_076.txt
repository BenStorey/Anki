Nuance_EN: A complete sentence meaning "He is tall." Focuses on the person's height. Common in everyday description.
Nuance_CN: 完整的句子，表示“他的身高很高”。日常描述中常用。
Example1: 他个子很高，站在人群里很显眼。
Example1_EN: He is tall and stands out in a crowd.
Example2: 他个子很高，所以总是被选去当旗手。
Example2_EN: He is tall, so he is always chosen to be the flag bearer.
Example3: 他个子很高，但性格很温和。
Example3_EN: He is tall, but his personality is very gentle.

===word: 抢===
Meaning: to rob, to compete for
Nuance_EN: Can mean to physically take something by force (rob) or to compete for an opportunity or item (snatch, scramble). Often implies urgency or aggression.
Nuance_CN: 可指用暴力夺取（抢劫），也可指争先获取（抢购、抢时间）。常带有紧迫或争夺的意味。
Example1: 有人抢了她的包，她赶紧报警。
Example1_EN: Someone snatched her bag, and she quickly called the police.
Example2: 双十一大家都在抢购打折商品。
Example2_EN: On Singles' Day, everyone is scrambling to buy discounted items.
Example3: 别抢，一个一个来！
Example3_EN: Don't push; take turns!

===word: 熟悉===
Meaning: to know well, to be familiar with
Nuance_EN: Indicates a deep understanding or familiarity with a person, place, topic, or skill. Opposite of 陌生 (unfamiliar).
Nuance_CN: 表示对某人、某地、某事或技能非常了解，不陌生。与“陌生”相对。
Example1: 我对这条路很熟悉，闭着眼都能走。
Example1_EN: I'm very familiar with this road; I could walk it with my eyes closed.
Example2: 他熟悉多种编程语言。
Example2_EN: He is familiar with multiple programming languages.
Example3: 我们刚认识，还不太熟悉。
Example3_EN: We just met, so we're not very familiar yet.

===word: 过分===

Nuance_EN: Describes behavior or demands that exceed reasonable limits, often with a negative connotation. Can also be used as an adverb meaning "excessively."
Nuance_CN: 指言行或要求超出合理范围，常含批评意味。也可作副词表示“过度地”。
Example1: 你这样做太过分了，完全不考虑别人的感受。
Example1_EN: What you did is going too far; you completely ignored others' feelings.
Example2: 他过分谦虚，反而让人觉得虚伪。
Example2_EN: He is excessively modest, which makes him seem insincere.
Example3: 别过分依赖别人，要学会独立。
Example3_EN: Don't rely too much on others; learn to be independent.

===word: 了===
Meaning: to end, to finish
Nuance_EN: As a verb (liǎo), it means to end, finish, or settle. Also used in phrases like 了结 (to conclude) or 受不了 (can't stand). Note: This is the verb form, not the aspect particle.
Nuance_CN: 作动词（liǎo）时表示结束、完毕或解决。也用于“了结”“受不了”等短语。注意与助词“了（le）”区分。
Example1: 这件事终于了了，我们可以放心了。
Example1_EN: This matter is finally settled; we can rest assured.
Example2: 他受不了这么大的压力。
Example2_EN: He can't stand such great pressure.
Example3: 一了百了，干脆把问题彻底解决。
Example3_EN: Once and for all, just solve the problem completely.

===word: 道理===
Meaning: principle, reason
Nuance_EN: Refers to a logical principle, truth, or reasoning behind something. Can also mean "sense" as in "讲道理" (to be reasonable).
Nuance_CN: 指事物内在的逻辑、真理或原因。也可指“情理”，如“讲道理”表示合理。
Example1: 你说的有道理，我同意你的看法。
Example1_EN: What you said makes sense; I agree with your view.
Example2: 做任何事都要讲道理，不能蛮干。
Example2_EN: You should be reasonable in everything you do, not act recklessly.
Example3: 这个道理很简单，人人都能理解。
Example3_EN: This principle is very simple; everyone can understand it.

===word: 入乡随俗===
Meaning: When in Rome, do as the Romans do
Nuance_EN: A Chinese idiom advising adaptation to local customs when visiting or living in a new place. Equivalent to the English proverb.
Nuance_CN: 中文成语，劝告人们到了新地方要遵守当地习俗。与英文谚语“When in Rome, do as the Romans do”对应。
Example1: 去国外留学，一定要入乡随俗，尊重当地文化。
Example1_EN: When studying abroad, you must do as the Romans do and respect the local culture.
Example2: 入乡随俗，我们也要学着吃当地的食物。
Example2_EN: When in Rome, do as the Romans do; we should also learn to eat local food.
Example3: 他刚来中国时不太习惯，但很快就入乡随俗了。
Example3_EN: He wasn't used to it when he first came to China, but he soon adapted to local customs.

Example2_EN: How this should be done is up to you.
Example3: 该来的总会来，别着急。
Example3_EN: What should come will come; don't worry.
