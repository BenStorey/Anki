Nuance_EN: 推理 is a verb meaning to deduce or reason logically, used in formal contexts like logic, mathematics, and detective work. It suggests analytical thinking.
Nuance_CN: 推理指根据已知事实进行逻辑推断，常用于逻辑学、数学和侦探故事中，强调理性分析。
Example1: 侦探通过线索推理出凶手是谁。
Example1_EN: The detective deduced who the murderer was from the clues.
Example2: 数学推理需要严密的逻辑。
Example2_EN: Mathematical reasoning requires strict logic.
Example3: 他善于推理，总能找到问题的答案。
Example3_EN: He is good at reasoning and always finds the answer to problems.

===word: 推论===
Meaning: Inference; deduction
Nuance_EN: 推论 is a noun referring to a logical conclusion drawn from evidence or premises. It is used in academic, legal, or everyday reasoning contexts.
Nuance_CN: 推论指根据前提或证据得出的逻辑结论，常用于学术、法律或日常讨论中，强调推理的结果。
Example1: 你的推论缺乏事实依据。
Example1_EN: Your inference lacks factual basis.
Example2: 科学的推论需要经过实验验证。
Example2_EN: Scientific deductions need to be verified by experiments.
Example3: 从这些数据可以得出一个重要的推论。
Example3_EN: An important inference can be drawn from these data.

===word: 唾弃===
Meaning: Despise and reject; spurn
Nuance_EN: 唾弃 literally means "to spit on and discard," expressing strong moral contempt and rejection. It is used in formal or literary contexts for actions or people considered disgraceful.
Nuance_CN: 唾弃字面意为“吐唾沫并丢弃”，表达极度的道德鄙视和排斥，常用于正式或文学语境，形容可耻行为。
Example1: 这种背叛行为被所有人唾弃。
Example1_EN: Such betrayal is despised and rejected by everyone.
Example2: 社会应该唾弃那些贪污腐败的官员。
Example2_EN: Society should spurn those corrupt officials.
Example3: 他的谎言最终使他遭到唾弃。
Example3_EN: His lies eventually led to his being spurned.

===word: 瓦解===
Meaning: Collapse; disintegrate
Nuance_EN: 瓦解 describes a complete and rapid breakdown of a structured system, organization, or entity, using the metaphor of disintegrating tiles. Often used for armies, regimes, or morale.
Nuance_CN: 瓦解比喻系统或组织的彻底崩溃，如土崩瓦解，常用于形容军队、政权或士气的迅速溃散。
Example1: 敌人的防线很快就瓦解了。
Example1_EN: The enemy's defense line quickly collapsed.
Example2: 公司内部分裂导致团队瓦解。
Example2_EN: Internal division within the company caused the team to disintegrate.
Example3: 谣言使他的人心彻底瓦解。
Example3_EN: The rumors completely shattered morale.

===word: 挽回===
Meaning: Salvage; retrieve; mend
Nuance_EN: 挽回 implies urgent, strategic action to reverse damage or restore something valuable, such as a reputation, relationship, or loss. It suggests active effort.
Nuance_CN: 挽回指通过努力扭转不利局面或恢复失去的东西，常用于挽回面子、感情或经济损失，强调主动性。
Example1: 他试图挽回这段破裂的友谊。
Example1_EN: He tried to mend the broken friendship.
Example2: 公司采取了一系列措施来挽回损失。
Example2_EN: The company took a series of measures to salvage the losses.
Example3: 现在道歉已经无法挽回局面了。
Example3_EN: Apologizing now can no longer retrieve the situation.

===word: 微观===
Meaning: Microscopic; micro-
Nuance_EN: 微观 refers to the small-scale level of observation, analysis, or phenomena, opposite of "宏观" (macro). It is a formal term used in science, economics, and social studies.
Nuance_CN: 微观指微观层面的观察或现象，与“宏观”相对，常用于科学、经济、社会学等领域，强调局部细节。
Example1: 微观经济学研究个体市场的运行。
Example1_EN: Microeconomics studies the operation of individual markets.
Example2: 通过显微镜可以观察微观世界。
Example2_EN: Microscopic worlds can be observed through a microscope.
Example3: 这个问题需要从微观角度分析。
Example3_EN: This issue needs to be analyzed from a micro perspective.

===word: 为期===

Nuance_CN: 为期指以一段时间为期限，常用于正式通知或计划中，表示活动、培训等的持续时间。
Example1: 培训为期两周，从下周一正式开始。
Example1_EN: The training lasts two weeks, starting next Monday.
Example2: 这次展览为期一个月。
Example2_EN: The exhibition runs for one month.
Example3: 合同为期三年，到期可以续签。
Example3_EN: The contract is for three years and can be renewed upon expiration.

===word: 文凭===
Meaning: Diploma
Nuance_EN: 文凭 refers to an official document certifying completion of an educational program, such as a university degree. It is important for job applications and further study.
Nuance_CN: 文凭是证明学历的官方文件，如大学毕业证书，常用于求职和升学，反映教育背景。
Example1: 他花了很多年才拿到大学文凭。
Example1_EN: He spent many years earning a university diploma.
Example2: 现在很多工作都要求本科以上文凭。
Example2_EN: Many jobs now require a bachelor's degree or higher.
Example3: 文凭虽然重要，但能力更关键。
Example3_EN: Although a diploma is important, ability is more crucial.
