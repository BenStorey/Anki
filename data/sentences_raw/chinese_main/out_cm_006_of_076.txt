Nuance_EN: Literally "to weigh body weight". Used when checking your own weight on a scale.
Nuance_CN: 字面意思是“称量身体重量”。用于在秤上检查自己的体重。
Example1: 我每天早上都要称体重。
Example1_EN: I weigh myself every morning.
Example2: 别吃完饭就称体重。
Example2_EN: Don't weigh yourself right after eating.
Example3: 她最近在减肥，天天称体重。
Example3_EN: She's on a diet lately and weighs herself every day.

===word: 轻===
Meaning: light (weight)
Nuance_EN: Opposite of heavy. Can describe physical weight, or figurative lightness like a light sleep.
Nuance_CN: 与“重”相对。可描述实际重量，也可比喻轻的程度，如轻轻的声音。
Example1: 这个箱子很轻。
Example1_EN: This box is very light.
Example2: 她轻轻地关上了门。
Example2_EN: She gently closed the door.
Example3: 我睡得比较轻，容易醒。
Example3_EN: I sleep lightly and wake up easily.

===word: 吸毒===
Meaning: to take drugs (narcotics)
Nuance_EN: Refers to illegal drug use. Very negative and serious in tone. Not used for smoking cigarettes.
Nuance_CN: 指非法使用毒品。语气非常负面和严肃。不用于吸烟。
Example1: 吸毒对身体伤害很大。
Example1_EN: Taking drugs does great harm to the body.
Example2: 他因为吸毒被抓了。
Example2_EN: He was arrested for drug use.
Example3: 我们要远离吸毒。
Example3_EN: We must stay away from drug use.

===word: 在路上===
Meaning: on the way; en route
Nuance_EN: Indicates being in the middle of a journey or trip. Can be literal or metaphorical.
Nuance_CN: 表示在旅途或行程中。可以是字面或比喻意义。
Example1: 我现在在路上，马上到。
Example1_EN: I'm on the way now and will arrive soon.
Example2: 我们在路上遇到了堵车。
Example2_EN: We encountered a traffic jam on the way.
Example3: 成功总是在路上需要坚持。
Example3_EN: Success always needs persistence on the journey.

===word: 理解错了===
Meaning: misunderstood
Nuance_EN: A verb phrase meaning you understood something incorrectly. Often followed by context of the mistake.
Nuance_CN: 表示你错误地理解了某事。常后接错误的语境。
Example1: 对不起，我理解错了你的意思。
Example1_EN: Sorry, I misunderstood your meaning.
Example2: 他理解错了老师的问题。
Example2_EN: He misunderstood the teacher's question.
Example3: 我可能理解错了这句话。
Example3_EN: I might have misunderstood this sentence.

===word: 我走错了===
Meaning: I went the wrong way
Nuance_EN: A complete sentence meaning you took the wrong path or went to the wrong place. Often said when lost.
Nuance_CN: 完整的句子，表示走错了路或去错了地方。常在迷路时说。
Example1: 我走错了，这条路不对。
Example1_EN: I went the wrong way; this road isn't right.
Example2: 走了一会儿才发现我走错了。
Example2_EN: After walking a while, I realized I had gone the wrong way.
Example3: 对不起，我走错了地方。
Example3_EN: Sorry, I went to the wrong place.
