Nuance_EN: Refers to an artificial eye used to replace a lost eye due to accident or disease. Made of glass or acrylic, focusing on natural appearance. A medical term, not restoring vision but reducing visual discomfort.

===word: 義肢===
Meaning: Prosthetic limb
Nuance_EN: An artificial limb attached to replace a lost arm or leg due to accident or disease. Advanced technology allows fine work with prosthetic hands, and users can enjoy activities like marathons.

===word: 義賊===
Meaning: Robin Hood / Chivalrous thief
Nuance_EN: Pronounced "ぎぞく," it refers to a thief who steals for the weak, not for personal gain. Examples include Japan's "鼠小僧" and England's Robin Hood, seen as heroic figures.

===word: 羽===
Meaning: Feather / Wing
Nuance_EN: Refers to the flight organ on birds, including covering feathers. Also used as a counter for birds (一羽, 二羽). The reading "はね" is common.

===word: 羽ばたく===
Meaning: Flap wings
Nuance_EN: Refers to the action of birds or winged creatures moving their wings up and down to fly. Used metaphorically for starting fresh or becoming free, with a positive image of strength and liberation.

===word: 羽交い締め===
Meaning: Arm lock (from behind)
Nuance_EN: A restraint method where both arms are pinned behind the back from behind. Used in police arrests or breaking up fights, appearing in news or action scenes. Rare in daily conversation.

===word: 羽化===
Meaning: Emergence (from pupa)
Nuance_EN: The process of a larva or pupa becoming an adult with wings. A biological term for insect development.

===word: 羽団扇===
Meaning: Feather fan
Nuance_EN: A traditional fan made of bird feathers, used in Japanese traditional performing arts like Noh. More formal than ordinary fans, also used in rituals and ceremonies.

===word: 羽振り===
Meaning: Influence / Prosperity
Nuance_EN: Derived from a bird flapping its wings, it means being financially well-off and in high spirits.

===word: 羽毛===
Meaning: Feathers / Down
Nuance_EN: Refers to the soft, fine feathers covering a bird's body. Known for insulation, used in products like down comforters and jackets.

===word: 羽目になる===
Meaning: Get stuck in / End up in
Nuance_EN: A colloquial expression meaning to fall into an undesirable situation or be forced into an unpleasant role.

===word: 羽目をはずす===
Meaning: Let loose / Go overboard
Nuance_EN: Means to get carried away with fun and lose restraint, going beyond proper limits.

===word: 羽織る===
Meaning: Throw on (a jacket)
Nuance_EN: Refers to the action of lightly draping a jacket or coat over the shoulders, rather than putting arms through sleeves.

===word: 羽音===
Meaning: Sound of wings
Nuance_EN: The sound of birds or insects flapping their wings, like a faint buzzing.

===word: 翌（日、朝、年）===
Meaning: Next (day, morning, year)
Nuance_EN: A prefix meaning the day or year following something. Used in forms like "翌日," "翌年," "翌朝."

===word: 習俗===
Meaning: Customs / Mores
Nuance_EN: Long-standing habits and customs in society. A formal word used in academic or folkloric contexts.

===word: 習字===
Meaning: Penmanship / Calligraphy practice
Nuance_EN: Refers to practicing writing characters beautifully with a brush. Part of Japanese school education and a popular children's activity. More practical than "書道," which is more artistic.

===word: 習得する===
Meaning: Acquire / Master
Nuance_EN: Means to gain knowledge or skills through study and practice. Emphasizes the result of being able to use what was learned, like language or skill acquisition.

===word: 習性===
Meaning: Habit / Trait
Nuance_EN: Refers to innate behavioral tendencies or habitual patterns in living organisms. Describes natural behavior in animals and humans.

===word: 習慣===
Meaning: Habit / Custom
Nuance_EN: Refers to patterns of behavior or thinking acquired through long-term repetition. Used for personal habits and societal customs, neutral for both good and bad habits.

===word: 習慣は国によって違う===
Meaning: Customs differ by country
Nuance_EN: An example sentence using "習慣" to mean customs that vary between countries.

===word: 習熟===
Meaning: Proficiency / Mastery
Nuance_EN: The state of becoming skilled through repeated practice and study. Refers to being well-versed in a skill or knowledge.

===word: 翡翠===
Meaning: Jadeite / Kingfisher
Nuance_EN: A beautiful green gemstone used in accessories and ornaments, highly valued in East Asia. The same kanji can also refer to the kingfisher bird.

===word: 翩翻===
Meaning: Fluttering / Swaying
Nuance_EN: A highly literary onomatopoeic word describing something fluttering lightly in the wind, like butterflies or flags. Rare in modern conversation, found in classical poetry.

===word: 翳す===
Meaning: Hold up / Shade
Nuance_EN: Means to hold a hand or object up in front of the face or over the head. Used for physical actions like shading from the sun or waving a fan.

===word: 翳り===
Meaning: Shadow / Dimness
Nuance_EN: Refers to literal shadow or, metaphorically, a dark sign or gloom appearing in things. A literary expression with nuances of fading hope or vitality.

===word: 翻す===
Meaning: Flip / Reverse
Nuance_EN: Means to turn over, change direction, or suddenly change one's attitude or opinion. A somewhat formal expression, used in literature and metaphor.

===word: 翻弄===
Meaning: Toss about / Toy with
Nuance_EN: Means to manipulate or toy with someone at will, or to be tossed about by fate or circumstances. Often used in passive situations.

===word: 翻意===
Meaning: Change of mind
Nuance_EN: Refers to changing one's intention or opinion, often in a formal context.

===word: 翻案===
Meaning: Adaptation
Nuance_EN: Means to adapt an existing novel, play, or film into a different form or setting. Not just translation, but reinterpreting the original while changing characters or settings.

===word: 翻筋斗===
Meaning: Somersault
Nuance_EN: Refers to an acrobatic move rotating the body in the air, like a backflip. Used in the phrase "もんどりを打つ" for surprise or falling over. Somewhat old-fashioned.

===word: 翻訳===
Meaning: Translation
Nuance_EN: The act of converting written text from one language to another. Contrasts with interpretation, which handles spoken language.

===word: 翻訳家===
Meaning: Translator
Nuance_EN: A person whose profession is translating texts between different languages.

===word: 翼===
Meaning: Wing
Nuance_EN: Refers to the part extending from the body of a bird or airplane for flight. Bird wings are covered in feathers; airplane wings generate lift.

===word: 老いぼれ===
Meaning: Old fogey / Senile person
Nuance_EN: A somewhat insulting term for an elderly person whose mental and physical functions have declined. Can be used self-deprecatingly but is rude when used for others.

===word: 老いぼれる===
Meaning: Become senile / Grow feeble
Nuance_EN: Means to become physically and mentally weak with age. Has a negative nuance, often used when lamenting one's own or others' aging.

===word: 老いる===
Meaning: Age / Grow old
Nuance_EN: An intransitive verb meaning to age and decline in body and mind. More literary and serious than "年を取る," implying an unavoidable natural process.

===word: 老ける===
Meaning: Look old / Age
Nuance_EN: Means to look or become older in appearance or bodily function. Not just aging, but looking older than one's actual age. Used when discussing changes in looks or health.

===word: 老け込む===
Meaning: Become aged-looking
Nuance_EN: Means to come to look older than one's actual age, or to enter a state of aged appearance and physical decline.

===word: 老け顔===
Meaning: Old-looking face
Nuance_EN: A colloquial term for a face that looks older than the actual age, like a young person appearing mature.

===word: 老人===
Meaning: Elderly person
Nuance_EN: Refers to an aged person, especially the elderly. "高齢者" is a more polite term.

===word: 老人が、何か言いたげに近づいてきた===
Meaning: An elderly person approached as if wanting to say something
Nuance_EN: An example sentence using "老人" in a descriptive context.

===word: 老化する===
Meaning: Age / Senesce
Nuance_EN: Means the decline of bodily functions as an organism gets older. A natural phenomenon, but often discussed negatively in beauty and health contexts.

===word: 老婆心===
Meaning: Motherly concern / Unsolicited advice
Nuance_EN: A word implying an elderly person's kindness or worry, with a nuance that the advice might be meddlesome. Used in the phrase "老婆心ながら" when giving advice humbly.

===word: 老婦===
Meaning: Elderly woman
Nuance_EN: A somewhat formal term for an elderly woman. More literary than "おばあさん," used in novels or newspaper articles. Can be disrespectful if used directly.

===word: 老害===
Meaning: Harmful elderly / Gerontocracy
Nuance_EN: A highly negative word for elderly people whose actions hinder younger generations. A very strong criticism.

===word: 老後===
Meaning: Old age / Retirement years
Nuance_EN: Refers to the period of life after retiring from work. Often discussed in contexts of pensions, care, and health, appearing in life planning and future concerns.

===word: 老後は妻と二人っきりの生活を楽しもうと思う。===
Meaning: I think I'll enjoy a life alone with my wife in old age
Nuance_EN: An example sentence using "老後" to talk about retirement plans.

===word: 老成===
Meaning: Mature / Precocious
Nuance_EN: Means being calm and discerning beyond one's age. A positive evaluation, but can imply a slightly cold impression if overdone.

===word: 老朽化===
Meaning: Deterioration / Obsolescence
Nuance_EN: Refers to buildings or equipment becoming old and losing functionality. Common in discussions of social infrastructure.

===word: 老朽化した===
Meaning: Deteriorated / Dilapidated
Nuance_EN: Describes a state where buildings or equipment have aged and degraded, requiring repair or replacement. Somewhat formal.

===word: 老獪===
Meaning: Cunning / Crafty (old person)
Nuance_EN: Describes an elderly person who is sly and shrewd. Implies cunning and calculation from experience, with a somewhat negative evaluation.

===word: 老眼===
Meaning: Presbyopia / Farsightedness (age-related)
Nuance_EN: Refers to the condition where the eye's lens loses flexibility with age, making it hard to focus on nearby objects. Common after 40, corrected with reading glasses.

===word: 老眼が進んで眼鏡なしでは近くの字が読めないので、不便ったらない。===
Meaning: My presbyopia has progressed so I can't read close text without glasses, it's so inconvenient
Nuance_EN: An example sentence using "老眼" and "眼鏡" in a complaint about aging.

===word: 老練===
Meaning: Experienced / Veteran
Nuance_EN: Describes being highly skilled and judicious from long experience. A positive term evaluating high expertise.

===word: 老舗===
Meaning: Long-established shop / Old store
Nuance_EN: Refers to a shop or company with a long history, built on tradition and trust. Implies not just age but inherited skill, taste, and reliability.

===word: 老若男女===
Meaning: People of all ages and genders
Nuance_EN: A four-character idiom meaning everyone regardless of age or gender. Used in formal settings and speeches.

===word: 老衰===
Meaning: Senility / Death from old age
Nuance_EN: Natural decline of bodily functions due to age. Dying from old age rather than disease is called "老衰死."
