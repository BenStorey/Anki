Nuance_EN: One of the three major religions of China; based on Laozi and Zhuangzi, emphasizing immortality and harmony with nature. Pronounced "Daojiao" in Chinese.

===word: 道楽===

===word: 道理に適う===

Nuance_EN: An idiom meaning something fits the logic and reasoning of things and is convincing; used when a thought or action is rational and correct.

===word: 道程===

Nuance_EN: Read "どうてい"; refers to physical distance to a destination, or the process/route to achieving a goal. A stiff expression also used metaphorically.

===word: 道端===

===word: 道義心===

===word: 道義的===

===word: 道草===
Meaning: Loitering / Dawdling
Nuance_EN: Stopping on the way to one's destination to waste time, literally "roadside grass"; metaphorically used as "道草を食う" meaning to loiter and play instead of reaching a goal. Often used for children returning home late from school. Also in warnings like "道草を食うな".

===word: 道路===

===word: 道路標識===
