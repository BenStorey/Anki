Nuance_EN: One who stands at the top in competition or battle, overwhelming others. Implies the image of a champion or supreme ruler.

===word: 覇道===
Meaning: Rule by force / Hegemony
Nuance_EN: A method of governing or dominating others through military force or power. From Chinese political thought, opposite of "王道" (rule by virtue). Carries a slightly negative nuance of coercive leadership.

===word: 見いだす===
Meaning: Discover / Find
Nuance_EN: To find something previously unseen through exploration or observation. A somewhat stiff verb, often used for intellectual or spiritual discoveries like "possibilities" or "purpose." Also written as "見出す."

===word: 見え見え===
Meaning: Obvious / Transparent
Nuance_EN: A colloquial expression describing a situation where someone's intentions or schemes are clearly unconcealed. Carries a critical or sarcastic tone.

===word: 見え透く===
Meaning: See through / Transparent
Nuance_EN: That which is meant to be hidden is clearly obvious. Used when lies, deception, or strategies are so childish they are easily seen through.

===word: 見え隠れ===
Meaning: Appear and disappear / Peek out
Nuance_EN: The state of being partially visible and partially hidden, like through fog or trees. Also describes emotions flickering on someone's face.

===word: 見かけ===
Meaning: Appearance / Looks
Nuance_EN: The outward appearance or impression, often implying it may differ from the actual content or reality.

===word: 見かける===
Meaning: Catch sight of / Notice
Nuance_EN: To see someone or something by chance, not intentionally. Implies a casual, incidental encounter.

===word: 見かけ倒し===
Meaning: Deceptively impressive / All show
Nuance_EN: A noun meaning that the appearance is splendid but the substance is lacking. Criticizes something that looks good but is actually unremarkable. Common in casual conversation.

===word: 見す見す===
Meaning: Knowingly / With full knowledge
Nuance_EN: An adverb describing the act of watching something bad happen without intervening, while being fully aware. Implies regret or criticism, used in phrases like "見す見す逃す" (let slip right before one's eyes).

===word: 見ず知らず===
Meaning: Complete stranger / Unacquainted
Nuance_EN: A phrase describing having no acquaintance or knowledge of someone at all. Used in "見ず知らずの人" meaning a total stranger.

===word: 見ず知らずの人に挨拶された===
Meaning: I was greeted by a complete stranger.
Nuance_EN: A sentence describing an unexpected interaction with someone unknown.

===word: 見せかける===
Meaning: Fake / Pretend / Make appear
Nuance_EN: To make the appearance or impression different from reality, sometimes for deceit or sometimes for playful effect. Often used to make something look better than it is, like "高そうに見せかける" (make it look expensive). More about impression management than outright lying.

===word: 見せしめ===
Meaning: Example / Warning (deterrent)
Nuance_EN: An act of punishing or reprimanding someone as a warning to others. Often done publicly. Carries a negative nuance.

===word: 見せつける===

===word: 見せびらかす===
Meaning: Show off / Parade
Nuance_EN: To intentionally display something to attract attention or boast, wanting to show off.

===word: 見せ付ける===
Meaning: Show off / Impress
Nuance_EN: To conspicuously display one's abilities or possessions to others. Implies intent to make an impression, often with a negative connotation.

===word: 見せ所===
Meaning: Highlight / Showcase moment
Nuance_EN: A noun meaning the most splendid part, the point worth seeing, or "the chance to show one's skills." Used in sports, performances, or work, referring to the moment when someone's ability or charm shines.

===word: 見た目===
Meaning: Appearance / Looks
Nuance_EN: The outward appearance or impression from looking. Used for physical appearance of people or objects. Often contrasted with substance, e.g., "looks good but inside..."

===word: 見つめる===
Meaning: Stare / Gaze
Nuance_EN: To fix one's eyes steadily on a single object or person. More common than "注視," and more focused than "眺める." Can accompany various emotions like love, curiosity, or surprise.

===word: 見て見ぬ振り===

===word: 見とれる===

===word: 見どころ===
Meaning: Highlight / Point of interest
Nuance_EN: The most attractive or interesting part of something, such as a performance, event, or place. Worth seeing.

===word: 見なす===
Meaning: Regard as / Consider
Nuance_EN: To think of something as being in a certain state or position; to judge. Often used for formal recognition or determination.

===word: 見やる===
Meaning: Gaze at / Look toward
Nuance_EN: To look fixedly in a direction, or gaze into the distance. Often carries a sense of pondering. Somewhat literary.

===word: 見るに見かねる===

Nuance_EN: An idiomatic expression meaning you can't stand by idly while something bad is happening, so you intervene. Used out of sympathy or a sense of justice.

===word: 見る目===

Nuance_EN: The ability to judge the true nature of things or people. Used in phrases like "見る目がある" (have good judgment) or "見る目がない" (lack discernment).

===word: 見る見る===

===word: 見る間に===
Meaning: In the blink of an eye / Rapidly
Nuance_EN: "In no time at all" or "while watching," describing very fast change. Carries visual imagery of things progressing right before your eyes.

===word: 見上げる===

Nuance_EN: To look upward from below. Used both physically and metaphorically to express respect or admiration.

===word: 見下げる===

===word: 見下す===

Nuance_EN: To treat others as inferior and consider them lightly. Can be literal (look down from above) but usually metaphorical (disdain). Stronger than "侮る" (slight), involves overtly contemptuous attitude or words.

===word: 見下ろす===
Meaning: Look down on / Overlook
Nuance_EN: To look down from a high place, or to treat someone with contempt. Both physical superiority and psychological superiority are implied.

===word: 見入る===

===word: 見出し===

Nuance_EN: The title of a news article, web page, or section that briefly summarizes content. Designed to grab attention and provide quick understanding.

===word: 見出す===

Nuance_EN: To find something after careful searching, or to discover new value or meaning. A somewhat stiff expression.

===word: 見分け===

===word: 見切りを付ける===
