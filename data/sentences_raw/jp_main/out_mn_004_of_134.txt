===word: お目付け===
Meaning: Overseer / Supervisor
Nuance_EN: Refers to a person who watches over and monitors someone's behavior, or the act itself. Originally from "目をつける," it describes someone assigned as a guide/supervisor for those likely to cause trouble or for beginners. Used in situations like teachers supervising students on school trips or seniors overseeing new employees. Somewhat old-fashioned and familiar.

===word: お目見え===
Meaning: Audience / Debut
Nuance_EN: To appear before a noble or superior for the first time to pay respects. Also means a new product or work being publicly revealed for the first time. A formal, dignified expression.

===word: お目通り===
Meaning: Audience (with high-ranking person)
Nuance_EN: A very polite expression meaning to formally meet a person of high status, such as the Emperor or a company president. Used especially for audiences with the imperial family or top executives. Used when speaking respectfully of someone meeting a noble person, rather than for one's own meetings.

===word: お相子===
Meaning: Tie / Draw
Nuance_EN: A colloquial expression meaning a match ending in a draw, or debts being settled so neither side owes the other.

===word: お祓い===
Meaning: Purification / Exorcism
Nuance_EN: A Shinto ritual to cleanse or drive away evil. Ranges from formal ceremonies at shrines to charm-like practices.

===word: お神輿===
Meaning: Portable shrine
Nuance_EN: A sacred palanquin carried during shrine festivals. Many people carry it with shouts, parading energetically — a famous Japanese sight. It is said to carry the deity.

===word: お笑い芸人===
Meaning: Comedian
Nuance_EN: A noun referring to comedians or entertainers whose profession is making people laugh. A collective term including manzai duos, solo comedians, and sketch performers, active on TV variety shows. Commonly used in the Japanese entertainment industry.

===word: 使い===
Meaning: Errand / Use
Nuance_EN: Refers to being sent to deliver something or the role of doing so. Also means the act of using something. In the sentence context, it appears in "使いやすい" (easy to use).

===word: お節介===
Meaning: Meddling / Officiousness
Nuance_EN: Refers to interfering in others' affairs or being overly helpful beyond what is needed. Well-intentioned but sometimes resented.

===word: お節料理===
Meaning: New Year's cuisine
Nuance_EN: Traditional Japanese dishes eaten during the New Year, packed neatly in stacked lacquer boxes. Each dish carries auspicious meanings such as longevity, prosperity, and health.

===word: お粗末===
Meaning: Poor / Crude (humble)
Nuance_EN: Used to humbly describe something one has provided as being inferior. In the set phrase "お粗末さまでした," it apologizes for the poor quality of what was offered.

===word: 粥===
Meaning: Rice porridge
Nuance_EN: Rice or grains boiled soft in plenty of water — easy to digest. Eaten by invalids or as breakfast.

===word: お絞り===
Meaning: Wet towel
Nuance_EN: A damp, wrung-out towel served for wiping hands before meals. Commonly seen at Japanese restaurants.

===word: 菜食主義者===
Meaning: Vegetarian
Nuance_EN: A person who follows a vegetarian diet, abstaining from meat and sometimes other animal products. Used in discussions about diet and lifestyle.

===word: 腹===
Meaning: Belly / Stomach
Nuance_EN: The front part of the body between the chest and hips, containing the internal organs. The place where fullness and hunger are felt. Also used in idioms like "腹が立つ" (get angry) and "腹を決める" (make up one's mind).

===word: 腹===
Meaning: Belly / Stomach
Nuance_EN: The front part of the body between the chest and hips, containing the internal organs. The place where fullness and hunger are felt. Also used in idioms like "腹が立つ" (get angry) and "腹を決める" (make up one's mind).

===word: お膝元===
Meaning: Home base / Seat of power
Nuance_EN: Refers to the place where a superior person or authority resides, or a region under their strong influence. Originally meaning "by the lord's knee," it came to mean the headquarters or center of an organization. Used figuratively like "本社のお膝元." Slightly old-fashioned but still in use.

===word: お膳===
Meaning: Meal tray
Nuance_EN: A traditional Japanese individual meal tray with legs, used to serve one person's portion. Seen at ryokan and high-end Japanese restaurants today.

===word: お膳立て===
Meaning: Preparations / Arrangements
Nuance_EN: Literally "preparing the meal tray," figuratively meaning making advance preparations and arrangements so things go smoothly. Used in business and event planning contexts. Close to "下準備" or "根回し," but with a more concrete image of setting up the framework.

===word: お色直し===
Meaning: Costume change (wedding)
Nuance_EN: The tradition in Japanese weddings where the bride and groom change outfits during the reception — typically from kimono to Western dress, or changing the color of the uchikake. A traditional Japanese wedding custom.

===word: 好き===
Meaning: Liked / Favorite
Nuance_EN: A na-adjective expressing fondness or preference for someone or something. One of the most basic and frequently used words in Japanese conversation.

===word: 冷ます===
Meaning: Cool down
Nuance_EN: A transitive verb meaning to make something hot become cooler. Used for food, drinks, and also figuratively for emotions like anger ("怒りを冷ます").

===word: 潤滑油===
Meaning: Lubricant / Lubricating oil
Nuance_EN: Oil used to reduce friction between machine parts. Figuratively, it refers to something that makes human relationships or organizational operations run smoothly.

===word: お茶を濁す===
Meaning: Equivocate / Fudge
Nuance_EN: Rather than outright deceiving someone, it means giving vague answers or avoiding the core issue to paper over the situation. Often used in business when dodging difficult questions — not a frontal refusal, but sidestepping without causing offense.

===word: お茶汲み===
Meaning: Tea-serving / Menial task
Nuance_EN: The job of making and serving tea, traditionally done mainly by women in companies. Figuratively, it can refer to unimportant chores or low-ranking work.

===word: お茶目===
Meaning: Playful / Mischievous
Nuance_EN: Refers to cute pranks or charming mistakes, or a personality that does such things. Used for harmless, endearing blunders, conveying affection toward the subject.

===word: お菓子===
Meaning: Sweets / Snacks
Nuance_EN: A general term for sweet foods like cakes, cookies, and candy, as well as snack foods. Extremely common in daily conversation.

===word: お菓子===
Meaning: Sweets / Snacks
Nuance_EN: A general term for sweet foods like cakes, cookies, and candy, as well as snack foods. Extremely common in daily conversation.

===word: お萩===
Meaning: Ohagi (rice ball with bean paste)
Nuance_EN: A Japanese confection made of glutinous rice wrapped in sweet bean paste. Traditionally eaten during the autumn higan equinox; called "ぼたもち" in spring.

===word: お見知り置き===
Meaning: Please remember me
Nuance_EN: A somewhat old-fashioned, polite expression used in first meetings, meaning "please remember me." Often used by older people in formal greeting situations.

===word: 見舞い===
Meaning: Visit to the sick
Nuance_EN: Visiting someone who is ill or injured to encourage them, or giving them get-well gifts or money. Also used in "災いの見舞い" to mean misfortune suddenly befalling someone.

===word: お詫び===
Meaning: Apology
Nuance_EN: Conveying one's feelings of apology, or the words and actions that express it. A polite expression frequently used in business settings.

===word: お転婆===
Meaning: Tomboy
Nuance_EN: Describes a girl who is lively and boisterous. Used for energetic girls who act in boyish ways; not a negative word.

===word: お辞儀===
Meaning: Bow
Nuance_EN: A uniquely Japanese etiquette gesture of bending at the waist to show respect. The angle and duration convey different levels of respect.

===word: 差し支えない===
Meaning: No problem / Acceptable
Nuance_EN: An adjective-like expression meaning there is no problem or it is permissible. Used to modestly indicate permission or to convey that something causes no hindrance.

===word: お通じ===
Meaning: Bowel movement (polite)
Nuance_EN: A polite way to say "defecation." Formed by adding the beautifying prefix "お" to "通じ." Used euphemistically in daily conversation, when asking about health, or in medical interviews.

===word: お遊戯会===
Meaning: Children's recital
Nuance_EN: A performance event held at kindergartens or nursery schools where children present plays, songs, or dances for parents. A warm, seasonal event in early childhood education.

===word: 注ぐ===
Meaning: Pour
Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to pour a liquid into a container. Used for pouring drinks, water, etc. Also used figuratively like "力を注ぐ" (pour effort into something).

===word: 酢===
Meaning: Vinegar
Nuance_EN: A sour-tasting seasoning made by fermenting rice or grains. Widely used in Japanese cooking for sushi rice, vinegared dishes, and dressings.

===word: 海外旅行===
Meaning: Overseas travel
Nuance_EN: Travel to foreign countries. A common noun in everyday conversation, often contrasted with domestic travel (国内旅行).

===word: どころではない===
Meaning: Not the time for / Far from
Nuance_EN: An expression emphasizing that one is not in a situation or mood to do something. In "買い物どころではない," it means "I'm in no state to go shopping."

===word: 届ける===
Meaning: Deliver
Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to deliver something to a destination or person. Used for packages, documents, and also figuratively like "声を届ける" (get one's voice heard).

===word: お鉢が回ってくる===
Meaning: Turn comes around
Nuance_EN: An idiom meaning one's turn has come to take on a role or responsibility. Originates from the custom of passing a food bowl around at meals.

===word: お門違い===
Meaning: Wrong target / Mistaken address
Nuance_EN: Refers to directing a request, complaint, or action at the wrong person or place. Used when someone approaches the wrong party for something.

===word: お願い下げる===
Meaning: Withdraw a request
Nuance_EN: A polite way to express canceling or retracting a previously made request or wish. Used especially in business when communicating the withdrawal of a request to the other party.

===word: 逆上せる===
Meaning: Get dizzy / Flushed
Nuance_EN: Refers to becoming dizzy or light-headed from staying too long in a hot bath or from heat. Can also mean getting carried away or infatuated.

===word: かえって===
Meaning: On the contrary / Rather
Nuance_EN: An adverb indicating that an action or situation produces the opposite result from what was expected. Used in comparative or contrastive contexts.

===word: かかと===
Meaning: Heel
Nuance_EN: The back part of the sole of the foot. Also used for the heel of a shoe. A common body-part word in daily life.

===word: かかる===
Meaning: Take / Catch / Hang
Nuance_EN: A highly polysemous verb: to catch an illness, to take time or money, to receive a phone call, to span a bridge, etc. One of the more challenging verbs for Japanese learners due to its many meanings.

===word: かがり火===
Meaning: Bonfire / Beacon fire
Nuance_EN: A fire built in an iron basket outdoors. Used for attracting fish in fishing, or for festivals, ceremonies, and security purposes. More formal-sounding than a casual campfire.

===word: かき乱す===
Meaning: Disturb / Throw into disorder
Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to violently disrupt order or peace. Can be physical (stirring up) but is often used figuratively to mean disturbing one's peace of mind or social order. Carries a nuance of intentionally or suddenly creating chaos.

===word: かき氷===
Meaning: Shaved ice
Nuance_EN: A representative summer Japanese dessert. Ice is finely shaved and topped with syrup — flavors include matcha, strawberry, and lemon. Note that "氷" is normally read "こおり" but becomes "ごおり" in "かき氷." Popular at summer festival stalls.

===word: かき消す===
Meaning: Drown out / Erase
Nuance_EN: Means to forcefully eliminate sounds, smells, or feelings. Stronger than simply "消す," with a nuance of actively and completely canceling out existence. "かき" serves as an intensifying prefix.

===word: かき集める===
Meaning: Scrape together / Gather
Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to gather scattered things by raking them together. Used both physically and figuratively — scraping together money, information, or people through effort. Carries a nuance of desperation or urgency.

===word: かくかくしかじか===
Meaning: Such and such
Nuance_EN: A phrase used to convey the gist of something while omitting specific details. Functions similarly to "云々" (etcetera, and so on).

===word: かくの如く===
Meaning: In this way (literary)
Nuance_EN: A literary, archaic expression meaning "like this." Used in writing; almost never used in modern everyday conversation.

===word: かく言う===
Meaning: Saying this (literary)
Nuance_EN: A somewhat archaic, literary expression where the speaker refers to themselves, meaning "I who say this." Used in the form "かく言う私も" to apply a preceding opinion or criticism to oneself with humility or self-reference. Often seen in speeches and essays.

===word: かけ離れる===
Meaning: Far removed from
Nuance_EN: A verb indicating a large gap or difference between two things. Used for reality being far from ideals, goals far from one's abilities, etc. — states that deviate greatly from expectations or standards.

===word: かじりつく===
Meaning: Cling to / Bite into
Nuance_EN: To bite into something with one's teeth and hold on, or to stick to one spot and not leave. Ranges from physical biting to figurative attachment.

===word: かじる===
Meaning: Nibble / Bite
Nuance_EN: To bite off small pieces with one's teeth. Also used figuratively to mean only learning something superficially.

===word: かすりもしない===
Meaning: Not even close
Nuance_EN: A colloquial expression emphasizing that a prediction or guess is completely off the mark. From "かする" (to graze) in negative form — "doesn't even graze the prediction" → "completely wrong." Quite casual, conveying surprise or exasperation.

===word: かすり傷===
Meaning: Scratch / Graze
Nuance_EN: A shallow wound where the skin surface is lightly scraped. Also used figuratively for minor damage that isn't life-threatening, like "かすり傷程度のダメージ."

===word: かそこら===
Meaning: Or so / Approximately
Nuance_EN: An adverb-like expression indicating a rough quantity or degree. Attaches to nouns like "一時間かそこら" or "千円かそこら" to mean "about that much." A casual expression commonly used in conversation.

===word: かたきを討つ===
Meaning: Avenge
Nuance_EN: "討つ" is an archaic verb meaning to attack and defeat an enemy. Often used in period dramas and historical contexts, like "仇を討つ" (avenge someone) or "賊を討つ" (defeat rebels). Rarely used in daily conversation but important vocabulary.

===word: かたじけない===
Meaning: Much obliged (archaic)
Nuance_EN: An archaic expression conveying humble gratitude for a superior's kindness, feeling honored and thankful. Often heard in period dramas. Rare in modern usage.

===word: かち割る===
Meaning: Crack open / Smash
Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to forcefully strike and break something hard. Used for cracking ice, sugar, etc.

===word: かったるい===
Meaning: Sluggish / Tiresome
Nuance_EN: A colloquial expression meaning feeling heavy and lethargic, not wanting to do anything, or finding something a hassle. Accompanied by fatigue, sleepiness, or low motivation; commonly used in casual conversation.

===word: かっちり===
Meaning: Snugly / Precisely
Nuance_EN: An adverb/mimetic word describing things fitting together firmly with no gaps. Used physically (gears meshing, lids closing tightly) and figuratively (a packed schedule, strictly following rules). Positively evaluates tightness and precision.

===word: かっと===
Meaning: Flare up (in anger)
Nuance_EN: An adverb/mimetic word describing sudden anger exploding. Expresses intense rage where blood rushes to the head in an instant.

===word: 余計===
Meaning: Unnecessary / Extra
Nuance_EN: Means more than necessary or unnecessary. Can be used positively or negatively. Especially common in the idiom "余計なお世話" (unwanted meddling), with a critical nuance of doing unnecessary things.

===word: かっぱらう===
Meaning: Snatch / Swipe
Nuance_EN: A colloquial verb meaning to steal or take something quickly and sneakily. A rough, casual expression for grabbing something without permission.

===word: かと言って===
Meaning: Even so / But then
Nuance_EN: A connective expression meaning "that said" or "but on the other hand." Used to add a contrasting condition or exception to what was previously stated. A convenient expression used in both speech and writing.

===word: かなぐり捨てる===
Meaning: Cast off / Fling away
Nuance_EN: Means to roughly tear off and throw away clothing or restraints. Used both physically and figuratively — "常識をかなぐり捨てて" (casting off common sense) to act boldly.

===word: 休暇===
Meaning: Vacation / Leave
Nuance_EN: Refers to taking time off from work or school, or the period of that time off. Includes paid leave, summer vacation, childcare leave, etc., and is recommended to be taken deliberately.
