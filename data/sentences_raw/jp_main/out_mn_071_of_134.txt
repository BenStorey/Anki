Nuance_EN: An organization formed for specific political purposes or positions. Broader than political parties, including any group engaged in political activities. A formal term used in news and official documents.

===word: 政治家===
Meaning: Politician
Nuance_EN: A person involved in the governance of a country or region, including elected legislators and administrative leaders.

===word: 政治家は国民のために奉仕すべきだ。===
Meaning: (sentence, key word: 奉仕)
Nuance_EN: The word "奉仕" (service) means serving others selflessly. The sentence means "Politicians should serve the people."

===word: 政治離れ===
Meaning: Political apathy
Nuance_EN: A phenomenon, especially among young people, of losing interest in politics. Discussed as a social issue related to low voter turnout and indifference. A compound word often seen in news.

===word: 政界に入る===
Meaning: Enter politics
Nuance_EN: Means to begin a career as a politician.

===word: 政見===
Meaning: Political views
Nuance_EN: The opinions or views of a politician or party on political matters. A somewhat formal word, used for candidates' policy stances during elections.

===word: 政論===
Meaning: Political commentary / Theory
Nuance_EN: A somewhat formal word meaning opinions, arguments, or theories about politics. Used in newspapers and commentary contexts.

===word: 政財界===
Meaning: Political and business circles
Nuance_EN: Read as "せいざいかい," a combined term for the political world (政界) and the business world (財界). Often used in discussions of their collusion or relationship in Japan.

===word: 故===
Meaning: Reason / Cause
Nuance_EN: A Chinese-derived expression indicating reason or cause, meaning "because of" or "due to." Used in written language, formal speeches, etc. In daily conversation, "だから" or "そのため" are more common.

===word: 故あって===
Meaning: For certain reasons
Nuance_EN: A somewhat old-fashioned expression meaning "for some special reason or circumstance." Used to hint at a reason without specifying it.

===word: 故事来歴===
Meaning: History and origin
Nuance_EN: Refers to the origin and history of something, especially ancient traditions and anecdotes. A formal expression used for explaining the background of shrines, temples, or cultural properties.

===word: 故人===
Meaning: Deceased person
Nuance_EN: Refers to a person who has died, especially one known to the speaker or being discussed. Used in funerals, memorials, or reminiscences, with a respectful tone. "故人を偲ぶ" (remember the deceased).

===word: 故国===
Meaning: Homeland / Motherland
Nuance_EN: The country where one was born and raised. A literary expression, more common in contexts of nostalgia from afar. "母国" or "出身国" are more common in daily speech.

===word: 故意に===
Meaning: Deliberately / Intentionally
Nuance_EN: An adverb meaning to act intentionally. Used in legal terms (故意) and often in negative contexts, implying intent rather than accident or negligence.

===word: 故買===
Meaning: (possession of stolen goods)
Nuance_EN: (No nuance provided, but it means knowingly buying stolen goods.)

===word: 故郷===
Meaning: Hometown / Birthplace
Nuance_EN: The land where one was born and raised, or a place that feels like home. A word with emotional resonance.

===word: 故障する===
Meaning: Break down / Malfunction
Nuance_EN: A suru verb meaning that a machine or device stops functioning normally. Used for cars, computers, appliances, etc. Very common in daily conversation.

===word: 敏感な===
Meaning: Sensitive
Nuance_EN: A na-adjective meaning sensitive to external stimuli or changes. Used for skin sensitivity, political sensitivity, price sensitivity, etc.

===word: 敏捷===
Meaning: Agile / Quick
Nuance_EN: A Chinese-derived word describing swift and nimble movement of mind and body. Used for both physical speed and mental quickness.

===word: 敏腕===
Meaning: Capable / Able
Nuance_EN: (No nuance provided, but it means having excellent ability or talent, especially in business.)

===word: 敏腕を振るう===
Meaning: Exercise one's abilities
Nuance_EN: (No nuance provided, but the phrase means to demonstrate or use one's skills effectively.)

===word: 敏腕家===
Meaning: Capable person / Go-getter
Nuance_EN: A word for a person with outstanding talent or ability, especially in work. Common in business contexts. The adjective form "敏腕な" is more common than the noun "敏腕家."

===word: 敏速===
Meaning: Prompt / Quick
Nuance_EN: A formal adjective describing fast and nimble movement or response. Used in evaluative contexts like "敏速な対応" (prompt response). Similar to "俊敏" (agile).

===word: 救世主===
Meaning: Savior / Messiah
Nuance_EN: Refers to someone who saves people from suffering or difficulty. Religiously, it refers to figures like Christ, but in daily use it is metaphorical for someone who saves a critical situation.

===word: 救出===
Meaning: Rescue
Nuance_EN: The act of saving people from dangerous situations or trapped places. Common in disaster and accident contexts.

===word: 救出（する）===
Meaning: Rescue
Nuance_EN: Same as above.

===word: 救助===
Meaning: Rescue / Aid
Nuance_EN: The act of helping people in dangerous situations. Commonly used in disaster and accident contexts.

===word: 救助隊===
Meaning: Rescue team
Nuance_EN: A team organized to rescue people in dangerous situations from disasters or accidents. Includes fire rescue, mountain rescue, coast guard, etc., composed of trained professionals.

===word: 救命〜===
Meaning: Life-saving (prefix)
Nuance_EN: A prefix related to saving lives from life-threatening situations. Forms compounds like "救命胴衣" (life jacket) and "救命ボート" (lifeboat).

===word: 救命いかだ===
Meaning: Life raft
Nuance_EN: (No nuance provided, but it means a raft used for emergency evacuation from a ship.)

===word: 救援===
Meaning: Relief / Rescue
Nuance_EN: Helping people in danger or distress. A formal word used in disaster and accident contexts, often in compounds like "救援隊" (relief team) and "救援物資" (relief supplies).

===word: 救援隊は子供をしらみつぶしに捜した。===
Meaning: (sentence, key word: しらみつぶし)
Nuance_EN: The word "しらみつぶし" (systematic search) means searching thoroughly one by one, like combing through lice. The sentence means "The rescue team searched for the children systematically."

===word: 救済===
Meaning: Relief / Salvation
Nuance_EN: Helping people or organizations in difficulty to rescue them from their condition. Often used in legal, economic, and social contexts, with a formal tone. Examples: "貧困救済" (poverty relief), "債務者救済" (debtor relief).

===word: 敗れる===
Meaning: Lose / Be defeated
Nuance_EN: Means to lose in a battle or competition. Used in games, elections, wars, etc. Also used for expectations or plans not being realized.

===word: 敗北や失敗は人間を非常に卑屈にする。===
Meaning: (sentence, key word: 卑屈)
Nuance_EN: The word "卑屈" (servile/obsequious) means excessively undervaluing oneself and flattering others. A negative evaluation of someone who is overly submissive.

===word: 敗戦===
Meaning: Defeat (in war)
Nuance_EN: Losing in a war or competition. Especially used for war defeat, with historical weight in Japan referring to WWII. More serious than simple "負け," implying national or societal scale.

===word: 敗残兵===
Meaning: Surviving defeated soldiers
Nuance_EN: Soldiers who survived a battle defeat. A military term, also used metaphorically for remnants of a defeated organization. Somewhat archaic, appearing in historical texts.

===word: 敗色濃厚===
Meaning: Signs of defeat are strong
Nuance_EN: (No nuance provided, but it means the situation strongly indicates an impending defeat.)

===word: 敗血症===
Meaning: Sepsis / Blood poisoning
Nuance_EN: A serious medical condition where infection spreads into the bloodstream, causing severe inflammation. A formal medical term, rarely used in daily conversation.

===word: 敗訴===
Meaning: Loss of a lawsuit
Nuance_EN: A legal term meaning to receive an unfavorable judgment in court, i.e., losing a lawsuit. The opposite is "勝訴." Appears in news reports about litigation.

===word: 敗走===
Meaning: Rout / Flight
Nuance_EN: Fleeing after losing a battle or competition. Implies a miserable retreat, used for groups like armies or sports teams.

===word: 教え===

Nuance_EN: The act of imparting knowledge, skills, or morals, or the content thereof. Used for teachers, parents, or religious doctrines. A noun form of "教える," with a formal tone.

===word: 教えを乞う===

Nuance_EN: To respectfully ask someone to teach you. Used for masters or experts, showing a humble attitude.

===word: 教わる===

Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to learn knowledge or skills from a teacher or others. Similar to the passive of "教える," but with a more polite and humble nuance.

===word: 教卓===

Nuance_EN: The desk placed at the front of a classroom used by the teacher. Located on the podium, used for holding textbooks and materials during lessons.

===word: 教員===

Nuance_EN: A person who teaches at a school. More formal than "先生" (teacher), referring to the occupation.

===word: 教唆===

Nuance_EN: A legal term meaning to incite someone to commit a crime or evil act. Used in criminal law as "教唆犯" (instigator). Much stronger than simply "勧める" (recommend).

===word: 教官===
Meaning: Instructor / Trainer
Nuance_EN: A person who teaches or trains in specialized institutions like military, police, driving schools, or cram schools. More professional and practical than school "先生," with a formal tone.

===word: 教師===
Meaning: Teacher
Nuance_EN: The profession of teaching knowledge and skills to students at schools.

Meaning: (sentence, key word: 対)
Nuance_EN: The word "対" (against/to) indicates a relationship between two things. Used in "A対B" for matchups, comparisons, or ratios. Also a prefix in compounds like "対称" (symmetry) or "対立" (opposition).

===word: 教師に従順にせよ===
Meaning: (sentence, key word: 従順)
Nuance_EN: The word "従順" (obedient) means following orders meekly without resistance. Used for children, pets, subordinates, etc. Can be positive or negative depending on context.

===word: 教授はカナダ出身です。===
Meaning: (sentence, key word: 出身)
Nuance_EN: The word "出身" (origin) refers to one's birthplace, upbringing, or alma mater. Used in self-introductions and resumes. A very common word.

===word: 教本===

Nuance_EN: A textbook or manual for learning a specific field. More practical and specialized than school textbooks.

===word: 教材===
Meaning: Teaching materials
Nuance_EN: Materials and tools used for education and learning. Common in school and classroom contexts.

===word: 教条===
Meaning: Dogma
Nuance_EN: Read as "きょうじょう," meaning an absolute, unquestionable teaching or principle. Used in religion or ideology, often with a negative connotation of inflexibility. The adjective form "教条的" (dogmatic) is also common.

===word: 教皇===
Meaning: Pope
Nuance_EN: The supreme leader of the Catholic Church, the Roman Pontiff. Heads the worldwide Catholic faithful and governs Vatican City. Appears in religious contexts and international news.

===word: 教祖===
Meaning: Founder of a religion
Nuance_EN: The founder of a religious sect or denomination, the origin of its teachings. Can be used for founders of major religions, but in Japan often refers to new religious movements, with a slightly sensational nuance.

===word: 教科===
Meaning: School subject
Nuance_EN: The classification of subjects taught in school education, such as Japanese, math, science, social studies. A formal term in education systems, also common in daily conversation.

===word: 教科書===
Meaning: Textbook
Nuance_EN: A book edited and published for use in school classes, systematically summarizing learning content. Each subject has its own, generally approved by the Ministry of Education.

===word: 教練===
Meaning: Drill / Training
Nuance_EN: Strict, disciplined training mainly in military or martial arts. Evokes images of old Japanese military training, rarely used in modern education. Implies harsh, regimented instruction.

===word: 教義===
Meaning: Doctrine / Dogma
Nuance_EN: The systematized basic teachings and beliefs of a religion or ideology. Used for Christianity, Buddhism, Islam, etc., and also in political thought. A formal, academic term.

===word: 教習所===
Meaning: Driving school / Training school
Nuance_EN: A facility for teaching driving skills and qualifications. Typical example is an auto driving school.

===word: 教職===
Meaning: Teaching profession
Nuance_EN: The profession and duties of working as a teacher. Implies responsibility and commitment to educating children and students.

===word: 教育===
Meaning: Education
Nuance_EN: Teaching and nurturing knowledge, skills, and morals. A broad concept covering school, home, and social learning.

===word: 教育制度===
Meaning: Education system
Nuance_EN: The systematic framework for education established by a country or region, including school systems from elementary to university, curriculum standards, and entrance exam systems.

===word: 教育機関===
Meaning: Educational institution
Nuance_EN: A general term for facilities and organizations that provide education, such as schools, cram schools, and universities. Includes public and private institutions.

===word: 教訓===

Nuance_EN: A teaching or warning learned from experience or events. Contains wisdom or advice for good behavior or avoiding mistakes. Often used at the end of stories: "この話の教訓は..." (the moral of this story is...).
