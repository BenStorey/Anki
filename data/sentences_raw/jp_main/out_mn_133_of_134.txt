Nuance_EN: Artificial hair worn on the head, used for stage costumes, medical purposes, fashion, etc. Commonly written in hiragana as "かつら".

===word: 鬣===
Meaning: Mane
Nuance_EN: Refers to the long hair on the back of the neck of lions or horses. The lion's mane is a symbol of dignity and strength, often in documentaries. The horse's mane is called "たてがみ", valued for its beauty. Rare in daily conversation but essential in animal descriptions.

===word: 鬨===
Meaning: War cry / Battle shout
Nuance_EN: A shout raised to boost morale in battle, often used in the form "鬨の声".

===word: 鬩ぎ合う===
Meaning: Vie / Struggle
Nuance_EN: To fiercely compete or contend with each other, especially in a close contest. Used in business, sports, and political rivalries.

===word: 鬩ぐ===
Meaning: Strive / Compete
Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to compete or struggle, often in a tense or pressing situation.

===word: 鬱屈===
Meaning: Suppressed frustration / Gloom
Nuance_EN: Describes a state where negative emotions like dissatisfaction, anxiety, and anger accumulate inside without release. A somewhat formal term used in literature and psychology, depicting a heavy heart from unclear causes.

===word: 鬱憤===
Meaning: Pent-up resentment
Nuance_EN: Refers to long-held dissatisfaction, anger, or irritation that builds up due to inability to express directly to the other party. Often used in "鬱憤を晴らす" (vent frustration) or "鬱憤が溜まる" (pent up).

===word: 鬱積===
Meaning: Accumulated / Build-up (of emotions)
Nuance_EN: Indicates emotions like dissatisfaction, anger, and sadness that have accumulated over time without release, resulting in a heavy, sediment-like feeling in the heart.

===word: 鬱蒼===
Meaning: Dense / Overgrown (gloomy)
Nuance_EN: Describes a scene where vegetation is thick and dark, with little sunlight, creating a gloomy atmosphere. A literary expression used in scenery descriptions.

===word: 鬱陶しい===
Meaning: Gloomy / Irritating
Nuance_EN: An adjective describing psychological heaviness from cloudy weather, troublesome things, or irksome situations. Common daily word used for rainy season clouds, obstacles, annoying people, etc.

===word: 鬼===
Meaning: Demon / Ogre
Nuance_EN: A mythical creature with horns and fangs in Japanese folklore. Used metaphorically for something extremely strong or fierce, like "oni-like strength" or "oni face".

===word: 鬼ごっこ===
Meaning: Tag (game)
Nuance_EN: A children's game where the chosen "oni" chases and tries to tag others.

===word: 鬼気===
Meaning: Ominous aura / Eerie presence
Nuance_EN: Describes a terrifying, demon-like appearance or a chilling atmosphere. Often used in "鬼気迫る" (with overwhelming intensity), depicting unusual tension or horror.

===word: 鬼気迫る===
Meaning: Overwhelmingly intense
Nuance_EN: An expression meaning that a situation or person has a demonic intensity or eerie pressure, making one feel tense or frightened.

===word: 鬼畜===
Meaning: Brutal / Demonic (insult)
Nuance_EN: A strong derogatory term for a person who is cruel and inhuman like a demon or beast. Historically used in wartime propaganda ("demonic America and Britain"), now used metaphorically for extreme villains.

===word: 鬼神===
Meaning: Ferocious god/demon / Superhuman
Nuance_EN: Refers to a terrifying god or demon, or someone with superhuman power. Appears in mythology and also used metaphorically as "鬼神の如き" (like a demon god) to describe extraordinary strength or talent. A literary expression.

===word: 鬼門===
Meaning: Weak point / Taboo direction
Nuance_EN: Originally a term from Onmyodo referring to the northeast direction (unlucky direction). Metaphorically, it means something one is bad at or should avoid.

===word: 鬼面===
Meaning: Demon mask / Fierce face
Nuance_EN: A mask of a demon, or a fierce expression. Often used in "鬼面人を驚かす" (to frighten others with a fierce face).

===word: 魂消る===
Meaning: To be utterly terrified
Nuance_EN: A classical expression for being so shocked or frightened that one's soul seems to vanish, used in "魂消る思いだ" to emphasize extreme impact.

===word: 魂胆===
Meaning: Ulterior motive / Scheme
Nuance_EN: Refers to a hidden true intention or plot, often with a negative nuance of underlying malice or cunning.

===word: 魅する===
Meaning: Captivate / Charm
Nuance_EN: A verb meaning to strongly attract and fascinate someone, but with a gentler nuance than "魅了する" (enthrall). Mainly used in writing.

===word: 魅了===
Meaning: Enthrallment / Captivation
Nuance_EN: The state of completely attracting someone's heart and not letting go, used for charming people or artworks. Positive and indicates strong influence.

===word: 魅入る===
Meaning: Be bewitched / Fascinated
Nuance_EN: Means to be captivated or possessed by something's charm, often in a way that one cannot look away.

===word: 魅惑===
Meaning: Charm / Allure
Nuance_EN: Refers to a powerful attraction or beauty that captivates others. Used positively for people, scenery, or art, with a somewhat formal and literary tone.

===word: 魅惑的な声===
Meaning: Alluring voice
Nuance_EN: A beautiful or attractive voice that captures hearts, a compliment for a voice quality or manner of speaking that charms listeners.

===word: 魑魅魍魎===
Meaning: Monsters / Evil spirits
Nuance_EN: A collective term for various monsters and demons that dwell in mountains and rivers. All four kanji contain elements of "demon", giving an eerie feel. Metaphorically used to describe various villains or chaotic dark aspects of society, e.g., "a political world crawling with evil spirits".

===word: 魔が差す===
Meaning: Temptation gets the better of one
Nuance_EN: An idiom meaning to succumb to evil temptation and do something out of character, often used to explain momentary lapses or mistakes, with a nuance of self-excuse.

===word: 魔の手===
Meaning: Clutches of evil / Grasp of danger
Nuance_EN: A metaphor for the reach of evil influence, danger, or calamity. Used in expressions like "魔の手が伸びる" (the hand of evil extends) or "魔の手から逃れる" (escape from danger), often referring to criminal organizations or malevolent forces.

===word: 魔女===
Meaning: Witch
Nuance_EN: A woman believed to practice magic, frequently appearing in fairy tales and fantasy works.

===word: 魔導士===
Meaning: Mage / Sorcerer
Nuance_EN: Refers to a person who uses magic, often appearing in fantasy works, especially in games and anime.

===word: 魔性===
Meaning: Bewitching charm / Enigmatic allure
Nuance_EN: Refers to a mysterious charm or irresistible, bewitching beauty, often used to describe a woman's allure, with both positive and negative connotations.

===word: 魔手===
Meaning: Evil hand / Malevolent reach
Nuance_EN: A metaphor for a malicious hand or force that intends to cause harm, used in negative contexts like criminal organizations or conspiracies. A somewhat stiff expression.

===word: 魔改造===
Meaning: Extreme modification / "Demon" mod
Nuance_EN: Refers to modifying existing products or machines beyond their original design intent, often in extreme ways. Used among modification enthusiasts for cars, plastic models, appliances, etc.

===word: 魔法使い===
Meaning: Wizard / Magician
Nuance_EN: A person with the ability to use magic, well-known as characters in fairy tales and fantasy. Male wizards are sometimes distinguished from "魔女" (witch), but "魔法使い" can be gender-neutral.

===word: 魔法瓶===
Meaning: Vacuum flask / Thermos
Nuance_EN: A container that uses vacuum insulation to keep contents hot or cold. Though it includes "魔法" (magic), it's a practical item. In daily life, "水筒" (water bottle) or "ボトル" are more common; "魔法瓶" sounds somewhat old-fashioned or technical.

===word: 魔物===
Meaning: Monster / Fiend
Nuance_EN: Refers to supernatural beings like demons or evil spirits, often appearing in fantasy and horror. Considered unreal in reality, but featured in legends and stories.

===word: 魔除===
Meaning: Protective charm / Amulet
Nuance_EN: An object used to ward off evil spirits or bad luck, often placed at entrances or worn.

===word: 魘される===
Meaning: Have a nightmare / Be tormented by a dream
Nuance_EN: To have a nightmare or moan in sleep, accompanied by a feeling of being physically pressed down by a scary dream.

===word: 魚の皮を剥ぐ===
Meaning: Skin a fish
Nuance_EN: The cooking action of removing the skin from a fish, often done before grilling or simmering. "剥ぐ" implies peeling with force, also used generally for peeling skins.

===word: 魚の骨がのどに引っ掛かった。===
Meaning: Bone
Nuance_EN: Refers to the hard tissue supporting the body. Also used metaphorically for the core of a matter. Here it is the literal fish bone.

===word: 魚を切った後、まな板を洗ったの。===
Meaning: Cutting board
Nuance_EN: A board made of wood or plastic placed under food while cutting, a basic kitchen tool. Also known in the proverb "まな板の上の鯉" (a carp on a cutting board) meaning a helpless situation.

===word: 魚を水槽内で飼って行う研究は、長期的な観察が可能であることから、汎用性のあるデータが取得しやすい。===

Nuance_EN: A large container for water, used for keeping fish or for water storage.

===word: 魚介===

Nuance_EN: A general term for fish and shellfish combined, often used as "魚介類". "Seafood" or "シーフード" is more common in daily life, but "魚介" is used in somewhat formal or specialized contexts.

===word: 魚肉===
Meaning: Fish meat / Fish flesh
Nuance_EN: Refers to the flesh of fish, a food term used to distinguish from meat, used in nutrition and cooking.

===word: 魚雷===

Nuance_EN: An underwater weapon used to attack ships, mainly in military contexts, launched from submarines or torpedo boats.

===word: 鮃===

===word: 鮒===

Nuance_EN: A freshwater fish widely found in Japanese rivers and ponds, popular for fishing. Not common in everyday conversation.

===word: 鮫===

===word: 鮮やか===

===word: 鮮血===

===word: 鮮魚===

===word: 鯉===

Nuance_EN: A large freshwater fish, also kept as ornamental. In Japan, used for "koinobori" in Children's Day, and the "nishikigoi" is world-famous. Also homophone for "恋" (love), used in wordplay.

===word: 鯉のぼり===
Meaning: Carp streamer (Children's Day decoration)

===word: 鯔背===

===word: 鯖を読む===
Meaning: Falsify numbers / Cook the books
Nuance_EN: An idiom meaning to fudge numbers or age, originating from a pun between "saba" (mackerel) and "sabaku" (to handle/process). Often used for age exaggeration.

===word: 鯨===
Meaning: Whale
Nuance_EN: A large marine mammal. Japan has a long history of whaling, and whale meat has been consumed.

===word: 鯨飲馬食===

Nuance_EN: A four-character idiom meaning to consume huge amounts of food and drink, likening to a whale drinking water and a horse eating feed. Slightly humorous.

===word: 鯰===

===word: 鯱張る===

Nuance_EN: A unique verb describing a state of becoming tense and awkwardly formal, like a shachihoko (mythical fish-tiger roof ornament) standing stiff. Used with a light teasing tone for over-formality.

===word: 鰭===

===word: 鱗===
