Nuance_EN: The act of grasping the meaning or nature of something. Used in both intellectual and empathetic contexts.

===word: 悟る===
Meaning: To Perceive / To Realize
Nuance_EN: To deeply understand the essence or truth of something. Famous as a Buddhist term for enlightenment, but also used in everyday language as a more literary way of saying "to realize."

===word: 感謝===

===word: 将来===

===word: 予定===

===word: 意図===
