Example1_EN: He ratted out his accomplices to the police.
Example2: 匿名で垂れ込む人が増えています。
Example2_EN: More and more people are reporting crimes anonymously.
Example3: 誰かが彼の犯罪を垂れ込んだようです。
Example3_EN: It seems someone squealed about his crime.
