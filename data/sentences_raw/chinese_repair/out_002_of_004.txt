Example1_EN: We agreed to go hiking together this weekend.

Example2_EN: Many years after graduation, old classmates agreed to reunite in Shanghai.

Example3_EN: The two agreed to meet at the library entrance.

===word: 另类===
Meaning: unconventional; alternative
Nuance_EN: Describes people, styles, or ideas that differ noticeably from the mainstream. It can be neutral or imply eccentricity, depending on tone and context.
Nuance_CN: 指与众不同、偏离主流的人或事物。可能是中性评价，也可能带有“奇怪”或“非主流”的含义，要看具体语气。
Example1: 他的穿衣风格很另类。
Example1_EN: His dressing style is very unconventional.
Example2: 她喜欢听一些另类音乐。
Example2_EN: She likes listening to alternative music.
Example3: 在这所保守的学校里，他的想法显得很另类。
Example3_EN: In this conservative school, his ideas seem quite unconventional.
