Nuance_EN: An adverb used to emphasize the full amount or duration of something, often with a sense of surprise or abundance. Usually followed by a number or time.
Nuance_CN: 副词，强调数量或时间的充足，常带有意外或充裕的感觉，后面通常接数量或时间词语。
Example1: 他足足等了两个小时。
Example1_EN: He waited for a full two hours.
Example2: 这只西瓜足足有十斤重。
Example2_EN: This watermelon weighs a full ten jin (about 5 kg).
Example3: 她足足吃了三碗饭。
Example3_EN: She ate as many as three bowls of rice.
