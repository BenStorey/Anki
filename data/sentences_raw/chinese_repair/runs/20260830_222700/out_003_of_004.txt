Nuance_EN: Verb meaning to close the trading session. Can be used for stock markets or other markets. Also used metaphorically.
Nuance_CN: 指交易市场结束当天的交易活动。也可用于比喻义，如结束某项活动。
Example1: 股市在下午三点收盘。
Example1_EN: The stock market closes at 3 p.m.
Example2: 收盘后，他整理了当天的交易记录。
Example2_EN: After the market closed, he organized the day's trading records.
Example3: 这个项目还没有收盘，需要继续努力。
Example3_EN: This project hasn't closed yet; we need to keep working on it.

===word: 耐磨===
Meaning: wear-resistant, durable
Nuance_EN: Describes a material's ability to withstand wear and tear. Used for products like tires, shoes, coatings.
Nuance_CN: 形容材料能够抵抗磨损，保持性能，常用于轮胎、鞋底、涂层等产品。
Example1: 这种轮胎非常耐磨，可以行驶十万公里。
Example1_EN: This type of tire is very wear-resistant and can last 100,000 kilometers.
Example2: 耐磨材料广泛应用于工业制造。
Example2_EN: Wear-resistant materials are widely used in industrial manufacturing.
Example3: 他的鞋子有耐磨的橡胶底。
Example3_EN: His shoes have a durable rubber sole.

===word: 一概而论===
Meaning: lump together, treat as the same
Nuance_EN: An idiom meaning to treat different things as identical or to make a sweeping generalization. Usually used in negative form: "不能一概而论" (cannot be generalized).
Nuance_CN: 成语，指对不同的事物不加区别地同样对待，常用于否定式“不能一概而论”，表示需要具体分析。
Example1: 每个人性格不同，不能一概而论。
Example1_EN: Everyone has a different personality; you cannot lump them all together.
Example2: 这个问题很复杂，不能一概而论。
Example2_EN: This issue is very complex; it cannot be generalized.
Example3: 虽然都是电子产品，但价格和质量不能一概而论。
Example3_EN: Although they are all electronic products, their prices and qualities cannot be treated the same.

===word: 民脂民膏===
Meaning: people's hard-earned wealth
Nuance_EN: A set phrase meaning the wealth or fat of the people, often used to criticize corrupt officials who exploit the people. Literally "people's fat and grease".
Nuance_CN: 成语，指人民用血汗换来的财富，常用于批评贪官污吏剥削百姓。
Example1: 这些贪官利用职权搜刮民脂民膏。
Example1_EN: These corrupt officials used their power to extort the people's hard-earned wealth.
Example2: 政府应该保护民脂民膏，而不是浪费。
Example2_EN: The government should protect the people's wealth, not waste it.
Example3: 他呼吁官员不要挥霍民脂民膏。
Example3_EN: He called on officials not to squander the people's hard-earned money.

===word: 乐呵呵===
Meaning: cheerful, happy (manner)
Nuance_EN: Reduplicated adjective describing a state of being cheerfully happy, often with a smiling face. Informal, colloquial.
Nuance_CN: 形容词，形容人非常高兴、快乐的样子，常面带笑容，口语化表达。
Example1: 退休后，他每天都乐呵呵的。
Example1_EN: After retirement, he is cheerful every day.
Example2: 她接到礼物后，乐呵呵地笑了。
Example2_EN: After receiving the gift, she smiled happily.
Example3: 孩子们在公园里乐呵呵地玩耍。
Example3_EN: The children were playing happily in the park.
