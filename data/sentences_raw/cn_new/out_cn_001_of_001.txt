===word: 乡愁===
Meaning: homesickness, nostalgia for one's hometown
Nuance_EN: Often conveys a deep, poetic longing for one's native place, especially after a long absence or in a distant location. It is a common theme in Chinese literature and poetry.
Nuance_JP: 
Nuance_CN: 指远离家乡时对故乡的深切思念，常带有一种含蓄、忧郁的情感色彩，多见于文学作品和日常表达中。
Example1: 每当夜深人静，他总会因思念家乡而感到深深的乡愁。
Example1_EN: Every time in the quiet of the night, he would feel deep homesickness from missing his hometown.
Example2: 这张老照片勾起了我对故乡的无限乡愁。
Example2_EN: This old photo stirs up boundless nostalgia for my hometown.
Example3: 漂泊在外的游子，最怕的便是深夜里的那一缕乡愁。
Example3_EN: For a wanderer away from home, what they fear most is that thread of nostalgia in the dead of night.
